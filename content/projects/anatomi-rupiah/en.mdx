# Anatomi Rupiah

## The problem

Bank Indonesia teaches the public a "3D method" for checking banknotes: Dilihat, Diraba, Diterawang (look, touch, hold to the light). Almost everyone can recite the three words. Far fewer can say what a watermark physically is, why intaglio ink feels raised, or what sits inside a security thread. Public materials show what to look for, rarely why it works. A fourth check, kode tuna netra, the raised marks that let blind people identify a note by touch, is barely taught at all.

There is a legal catch. UU 7/2011 Pasal 24 ayat (1) prohibits imitating the rupiah, and a violation carries up to one year and a fine of up to Rp200 million. The exception covers educational and promotional use with the word "spesimen" applied. Ayat (2) separately bans distributing imitation notes. I treated both as engineering constraints. The job was to teach the mechanism without ever producing anything that could pass for a note.

## What I built

### I built the legal safeguards before any content

The first real work was legal groundwork and the SPESIMEN marking, before a single note was drawn. Adding legal guarantees after the visuals existed would have been far riskier. The mark is drawn into the shape data of every note diagram, in the same space as the note's outline. It survives screenshots, printing and any attempt to hide it with styling. The site has no way to produce an unmarked note-like image.

### The build fails if a drawing breaks the rules

Eight automated checks run against the rendered output before every build. They confirm there are no photos of banknotes anywhere in the repository, every diagram carries its SPESIMEN mark, every diagram stays smaller than an actual note, and no export can produce a full note. If one fails, nothing ships. I kept the diagrams simple and never photorealistic, because realism itself would defeat the legal exemption.

### I drew every mechanism as a diagram

Each security feature has a hand-drawn cross-section: watermark, security thread, intaglio, colour-shifting ink, rectoverso, microtext, UV ink and the tactile code. A diagram avoids the photo restriction and shows the mechanism better than a photo can. The colour-shift tilt demo and the UV toggle compute the light physics live in the browser, with a drawn fallback on devices that can't run them.

### Every claim cites Bank Indonesia, and the site never gives a verdict

Every fact about features, denominations, heroes and motifs is structured data that must cite a source, usually a Bank Indonesia publication. The build fails if one is missing. For the newest 2022 series, I re-derived the data from Bank Indonesia's seven implementing regulations. The site explains how to check a feature, never how to make one. It has no camera and no "is this real?" button, because that authority belongs to Bank Indonesia.

### Photos of people are allowed, photos of money are not

Photos were banned outright at first. I then opened one narrow exception for the heroes on each note and the motifs on the back, because a person or a flower is not currency. The check keeps the two cases apart, so photos of notes are still blocked. Each of the 26 licensed photographs carries its credit and source.

## Result

Anatomi Rupiah is live in Indonesian and English as a static site with no network calls at runtime. It covers 10 denominations: the 2022 series plus the three largest 2016 notes still in circulation. You can open any note, click a numbered marker, and see a magnified view beside the mechanism diagram. A guided walkthrough of the 3D method ends by admitting that touch can't be shown on a screen, and sends the reader to handle an actual note. The site states that it is a personal educational project relying on the exemption in Pasal 24 ayat (1), and that it can't judge whether any note is authentic. I built it in two days in August 2026.

## Under the hood

- Next.js 14 static export (`output: 'export'`) on GitHub Pages, strict TypeScript, React, Tailwind, Zod, Vitest with Testing Library, pnpm. Fonts are self-hosted through `next/font`. No analytics, no CDN fonts, no 3D, image-processing or ML library.
- `docs/bi-reproduction-guidance.md` records the BI reproduction guidance checked at the first milestone. Full-note downloads were cut outright, because a print-resolution note image edges toward the distribution that ayat (2) bans.
- The SPESIMEN mark is SVG path geometry in the same coordinate space as the note outline, not a CSS overlay.
- `pnpm compliance:check` runs eight checks: asset policy, SPESIMEN presence, size cap, loupe-region scope, hero-anatomy marking, export-surface limits, dependency policy and source policy. `content:validate` rejects any claim without a citation.
- Figure and motif photos (6 figures, 20 motifs, Wikimedia Commons public domain or CC-BY-SA) are hash-pinned like brand assets.
- The optics demos are raw WebGL with GLSL fragment shaders computing thin-film interference and light transmission on a plain quad. No note texture is uploaded to the GPU, since that would mean rasterising the SVG. Tilt follows device orientation on mobile and a slider elsewhere. A pedagogy pass shows the number the tilt colour comes from and discloses when the demo falls back to a simplification. Mechanism diagrams can be shared; the export-surface gate keeps full notes out.
- The 2022 series data is re-grounded on Peraturan Bank Indonesia 24/8 to 24/14/PBI/2022, which closed a gap in tactile-code coverage.
- Each of BI's checks keeps one hue sitewide: Dilihat teal, Diraba copper, Diterawang amber, UV violet.
- About 15 accessibility commits fixed skip-link focus, duplicate announcements, touch-target size, contrast and non-reflowing demo panels. A later SEO pass added canonical URLs, hreflang, per-page previews, sitemap and robots.
- CI gates deploy on compliance, content validation, typecheck, lint and tests. Code is MIT; content is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- About 11,000 lines of TypeScript. The largest part, about 3,900 lines in `lib/`, is compliance, schematic geometry, WebGL and the citation system. 13 test files (5 compliance, 8 content), 92 commits over 13 and 14 August 2026.
